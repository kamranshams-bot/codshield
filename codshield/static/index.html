How CODShield works

Online stores in Pakistan and the UAE share what happened to their cash-on-delivery orders, so each store can decide which orders need a confirmation call or prepayment.

What we keep

Only scrambled fingerprints of phone numbers, emails and addresses, which can't be turned back into the original details. We never store names. Stores report facts such as "refused at the door", never opinions about a person.

How a result is decided

Recent reports count more than old ones, and records are deleted after two years. Successful paid deliveries lower the risk. One store's reports alone can never mark someone as high risk.

Your rights

You can contest any record linked to your number on the Contest a record page. Records under review don't count against you.
